# Home Assistant dashboard

Use the cards, tabs, and navigation on your dashboard as you normally would in Home Assistant. Tap a card to control the device or see more detail, just like in the Home Assistant app or a browser.

![Home Assistant dashboard shown in HA View](dashboard-overview.png)

HA View's menu also gives you shortcuts to the main areas of your Home Assistant:

- **Devices** and **Entities** — everything connected to your Home Assistant, and the individual things it can read or control.
- **Scenes** and **Automations** — your saved scenes and the automations you have set up.
- **Cameras** — your camera feeds.

What appears in each list depends on your Home Assistant setup. To change the dashboard itself — its cards, layout, and views — use Home Assistant's dashboard editor; HA View shows the result full screen.
