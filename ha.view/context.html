State-based face rules

Show a face when an existing Home Assistant entity has a matching state.

For example, when binary_sensor.example is exactly on, show Clock 1. Use an entity that already exists in your Home Assistant.

Rules, checked from top to bottom

The first matching rule wins. Move rules up or down to change their priority. States must match exactly, including capitalization.

Advanced detailsConnection, priority and local storage

Requires the embedded dashboard to be signed in and connected. A manual face choice takes priority for 15 minutes, then state rules, then the daily schedule, then the selected clock. Rules apply only while idle, never during interaction, settings or face selection.

Missing or disconnected entities fall back to your automatic faces. Samples expire after 15 seconds.

Cancel, closing this window, or Escape discards unsaved changes.
